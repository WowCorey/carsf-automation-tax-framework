# Placeholder Policy

Status: prototype data-governance scaffolding.

CARSF V1.5 uses illustrative placeholders to test formula plumbing, report structure, and hostile-review pathways. Placeholder values must not be treated as calibrated Australian data.

## Rules

- Placeholder values must remain labelled as placeholders.
- Real calibration values must not be added without source, review status, date range, and legal/privacy clearance.
- Restricted or personal data must not be committed.
- Evidence summaries may refer to source categories but must not imply the evidence has been collected.
- Calibration reports may list required datasets but must not include real values.

## Required Review Before Real Data Use

- Legal and tax review.
- Treasury/ATO-style modelling governance.
- Privacy and secrecy review.
- Data-owner approval.
- Economic and behavioural-response review.

This policy is not legal advice, tax advice, Treasury guidance, ATO guidance, ABS validation, Fair Work validation, OECD/BEPS analysis, audit validation, forensic validation, or economic validation.
