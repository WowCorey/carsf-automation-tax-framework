# Household Weighting and Subgroup Aggregation Shell

Status: V1.5 prototype synthetic weighting layer.

## Purpose

This layer adds a controlled shell for applying synthetic placeholder weights to synthetic household distributional scenarios and summarising results by subgroup.

It does not use real household data, survey weights, ABS microdata, HILDA, Census, DSS / Services Australia records, ATO records, Treasury modelling, PBO costing, welfare data, income data, or personal data. It does not modify firm-level CARSF liability.

## What Exists

- `model/carsf/household_weights.py` validates synthetic household weights.
- `model/carsf/subgroups.py` assigns synthetic household scenarios to deterministic placeholder subgroups.
- `model/carsf/weighted_distributional.py` aggregates residual gaps and high/critical shock shares using synthetic weights.
- `model/carsf/household_calibration_shell.py` lists external data requirements for future calibration.
- Examples live under `examples/household_weighting/`.
- Reports are generated by `python scripts/run_household_weighting.py`.
- Streamlit page: `simulator/pages/17_Household_Weighting.py`.

## Run

```powershell
python scripts/run_household_weighting.py
```

Generated reports:

- `reports/household_weighting.md`
- `reports/household_weighting.json`

## Non-Claims

These are synthetic household weighting outputs only. They are not population estimates, real distributional modelling, ABS/HILDA/Census analysis, DSS/Services Australia modelling, Treasury modelling, PBO costing, welfare advice, eligibility law, legal advice, tax advice, or economic validation.

Synthetic weights are not representative. Subgroup aggregation is a plumbing and reporting shell only.

## Calibration Needs

Future calibration would require controlled external review and authorised data sources for household composition, income distribution, employment displacement, regional labour-market depth, housing costs, essential costs, savings/debt buffers, welfare/transfer baseline, re-employment timing, payment eligibility, survey weighting, and uncertainty methods.

Real household data is not allowed in this repository.

## Known Risks

- Synthetic weights may make subgroup tables look more population-like than they are.
- Highest-risk subgroup labels are deterministic sorting aids, not population findings.
- Zero-weight examples are intentionally not assessable and not representative.
- Subgroup filters are coarse placeholders and may hide overlap, exclusion, or leakage issues.
- Calibration requirements are listed but unmet.
