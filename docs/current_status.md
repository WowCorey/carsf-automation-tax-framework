# Current Status

Status: private research prototype.

## What Exists

- CARSF V1.4 source paper converted to Markdown.
- CARSF V1.5 working paper with upgrade markers.
- Prototype formula modules for QLC, OPFTE/HLE, AII, NLTG, AAVA, AEL, ARL, credits, caps, CARS-I, and CoverageRatio.
- Prototype sector schedules for automotive repair and logistics / warehousing.
- Six illustrative worked examples.
- AAVA deductibility taxonomy appendix.
- Hostile tests for formula bounds, numeric fail-closed handling, avoidance metadata, and prototype schedule hardening.
- Streamlit simulator.
- End-to-end example runner and generated reports.
- Executable prototype safe-harbour classification for small-business, worker-assist/admin AI, startup, low-NLTG, and essential-services review pathways.
- Executable prototype anti-avoidance heuristics for token oversight, fake QLC inflation, offshore automation services, related-party AI fees, entity splitting, sector classification arbitrage, cloud-cost relabelling, robotics leasing, and artificial low AAVA.
- Prototype grouped-entity review flags for entity splitting, related operating entities, separated IP/platform/employer structures, and thin Australian employing entities.
- Prototype grouped-entity aggregation preview for illustrative related-entity structures.
- Mixed-activity / prototype apportionment preview for activity-share validation.
- Hybrid logistics stress variant with non-zero but intermediate NLTG.
- Non-operative transfer-pricing / related-party preview layer for offshore AI service fees, IP/platform royalties, cloud/inference relabelling, robotics leasing, service fees, data/model licences, cost-sharing, and other automation-linked related-party costs.
- Adjusted-AAVA preview calculations that do not replace reported AAVA or existing final liability.
- Mixed-unit handling that prohibits direct output/HLE aggregation where canonical output units differ and instead shows standalone liability sums, schedule-level comparison, and a value-weighted exposure index.
- Transfer-pricing and mixed-unit reports generated by `python scripts/run_examples.py`.
- Prototype evidence requirements layer for core formula inputs, safe harbour, avoidance, grouping, transfer-pricing, mixed-unit, and calibration fields.
- Prototype decision-log summaries for example, grouped, and transfer-pricing runs.
- Calibration shell listing future data requirements without real calibration values.
- Data source registry under `data/` with source categories only; no datasets are committed.

## What Is Not Yet Solved

- Real calibration with Treasury, ATO, ABS, DSS, HILDA, HELP, superannuation, state payroll tax, and industry data.
- Safe-harbour rules that modify liability.
- Legal grouped-entity aggregation.
- Legal/tax-law multi-schedule attribution.
- Related-party service charge adjustment.
- Offshore AI service attribution.
- OECD/BEPS analysis, ATO transfer-pricing practice, and legal treatment of related-party addbacks.
- Whether adjusted-AAVA previews should ever feed a liability calculation after legal and policy review.
- Whether mixed-unit activity can be converted through reviewed conversion metadata or must remain schedule-level only.
- Whether evidence requirements are too broad, too narrow, or insufficiently tied to actual administrative powers.
- Whether decision logs omit review steps that matter for hostile audit.
- How calibration governance should handle restricted datasets and privacy/secrecy constraints.
- Open-source AI treatment.
- R&D Tax Incentive interaction.
- Legal, privacy, international tax, trade, incidence, and behavioural elasticity review.

- Whether reports could be mistaken for real tax estimates despite warnings.
