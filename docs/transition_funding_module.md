# Transition Funding Module

Status: V1.5 prototype transition-payment funding layer.

## Purpose

The transition funding module previews whether placeholder automation revenue could support displaced-worker supplements, UBI-lite payments, transition income, retraining grants, automation-dividend style payments, or hybrid support packages.

It consumes fiscal trajectory outputs, but it does not modify firm-level CARSF liability.

## What Exists

- `model/carsf/transition_payments.py` for single-design placeholder payment costs.
- `model/carsf/payment_portfolio.py` for comparing payment portfolios.
- `model/carsf/transition_funding.py` for linking payment costs to fiscal trajectory years.
- `model/carsf/payment_sensitivity.py` for deterministic placeholder payment sensitivity sweeps.
- Examples under `examples/transition_payments/`.
- Reports generated by `python scripts/run_transition_funding.py`.
- Streamlit page `simulator/pages/14_Transition_Funding.py`.

## Run

```powershell
python scripts/run_transition_funding.py
```

Generated reports:

- `reports/transition_funding.md`
- `reports/transition_funding.json`

## Non-Claims

These are prototype transition-payment funding outputs only. They are not UBI policy, welfare advice, DSS modelling, Services Australia modelling, Treasury costing, PBO costing, legal advice, tax advice, or economic validation.

All payment amounts, participation rates, population bases, administrative costs, and retraining components are illustrative placeholders. No real welfare, DSS, Services Australia, Treasury, PBO, or budget calibration values have been added.

## Future Calibration Needs

Future work requires real welfare, population, labour-market, transition, fiscal, DSS / Services Australia, Treasury, PBO, legal, privacy, and economic review before any policy claim.
