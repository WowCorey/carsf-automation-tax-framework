# Evidence Requirements

Status: V1.5 prototype evidence-governance scaffolding.

## Purpose

The evidence layer defines what would need to be supplied before CARSF model inputs, review flags, or calibration fields could be treated as reviewable. It is designed for hostile review and audit-trail clarity, not for making legal or tax findings.

## Evidence Categories

- Core formula inputs: output, canonical unit, OPFTE_LIBC, QLC, AII, AAVA, FRV, capital base, rates, caps, and credits.
- Safe harbour: revenue thresholds, worker-assist/admin AI, low NLTG, startup status, and essential-service review.
- Avoidance: token oversight, fake QLC inflation, offshore AI services, related-party fees, cloud-cost relabelling, robotics leasing, entity splitting, and sector classification.
- Grouping: common control, IP ownership, platform/service-provider relationships, Australian customer-facing entity, Australian employer entity, and offshore service provider.
- Transfer pricing: agreements, invoices, royalty/licence agreements, cost-sharing, cloud/inference bills, robotics leasing contracts, data/model access fees, and technical service fees.
- Mixed units: canonical unit evidence, conversion metadata, activity shares, revenue/AAVA share basis, and schedule classification.

## Acceptable Evidence

Acceptable source categories include audited records, payroll and timesheets, contracts, system logs, invoices, schedule determinations, government datasets, business surveys, and independent legal/tax review. These are source categories only. The repository does not contain collected evidence or restricted datasets.

## What Remains Placeholder

All current worked examples and grouped examples remain illustrative placeholders. Their evidence assessments should remain `placeholder_only`, `insufficient`, or `partial` until real evidence is supplied and reviewed.

## Non-Claims

This evidence layer is not legal, tax, Treasury, ATO, evidentiary, forensic, audit, or economic validation. It does not validate liability or prove that any firm would owe tax.
