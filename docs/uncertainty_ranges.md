# Uncertainty Range Mechanics

Status: V1.5 prototype deterministic range layer.

## Purpose

This layer wraps synthetic household, distributional, and weighted subgroup outputs with deterministic low/base/high placeholder ranges.

It is not Monte Carlo, not forecasting, not calibration, and not statistical inference. It does not use real household data, welfare records, income records, ABS, HILDA, Census, DSS / Services Australia, ATO, Treasury, PBO, survey data, or personal data. It does not modify firm-level CARSF liability.

## What Exists

- `model/carsf/uncertainty_ranges.py` validates deterministic low/base/high ranges.
- `model/carsf/distributional_uncertainty.py` applies ranges to synthetic household scenario outputs.
- `model/carsf/weighted_uncertainty.py` applies ranges to weighted subgroup outputs.
- Weighted subgroup uncertainty preserves available subgroup metadata such as scenario count, synthetic weight, subgroup filters, matched scenarios, and unmatched scenarios.
- `model/carsf/uncertainty_summary.py` summarises stable, sensitive, and fragile signals.
- Examples live under `examples/uncertainty_ranges/`.
- Reports are generated by `python scripts/run_uncertainty_ranges.py`.
- Streamlit page: `simulator/pages/18_Uncertainty_Ranges.py`.

## Run

```powershell
python scripts/run_uncertainty_ranges.py
```

Generated reports:

- `reports/uncertainty_ranges.md`
- `reports/uncertainty_ranges.json`

## Non-Claims

These are deterministic placeholder uncertainty ranges only. They are not statistical confidence intervals, forecasts, real uncertainty quantification, population estimates, ABS/HILDA/Census analysis, DSS/Services Australia modelling, Treasury modelling, PBO costing, welfare advice, eligibility law, legal advice, tax advice, or economic validation.

Stable high-risk signals still require external validation. Fragile outputs require calibration before they should be interpreted.

## Calibration Needs

Future calibration would require controlled external datasets and methods for household composition, household costs, income recovery, re-employment timing, payment support, regional stress, subgroup weighting, uncertainty methods, and confidence treatment.

Real data remains outside this repository.

## Known Risks

- Low/base/high ranges may be mistaken for confidence intervals.
- Stable signals may be over-read as validated findings.
- Fragile outputs may be ignored if point estimates are easier to communicate.
- Range thresholds are placeholders and may be too narrow or too wide.
- Weighted subgroup uncertainty is not population uncertainty.
- Missing subgroup metadata is reported as a prototype warning rather than inferred.
