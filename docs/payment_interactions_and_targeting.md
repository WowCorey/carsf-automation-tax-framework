# Payment Interactions and Targeting Mechanics

Status: V1.5 prototype payment-interaction layer.

## Purpose

This layer previews how new automation-transition payments might interact with existing transfer baselines, targeting criteria, phase-in / phase-out rules, payment stacking, double-counting prevention, and placeholder support-payment fiscal incidence.

It does not design a real welfare program, UBI, Centrelink process, DSS model, Services Australia model, eligibility law, or budget costing. It does not modify firm-level CARSF liability.

## What Exists

- `model/carsf/transfer_baseline.py` separates existing transfer baseline costs from new transition payments.
- `model/carsf/targeting.py` estimates placeholder eligible populations and leakage / exclusion risk.
- `model/carsf/phase_rules.py` calculates placeholder phase-in, full, phase-out, and ended states.
- `model/carsf/payment_stack.py` previews payment-stack costs and mutual-exclusion double-count adjustments.
- `model/carsf/support_incidence.py` estimates household spending flow, placeholder GST recovery, and hardship offset previews without treating them as validated savings.
- `model/carsf/payment_interactions.py` combines these pieces into a single interaction result.
- Examples live under `examples/payment_interactions/`.
- Reports are generated by `python scripts/run_payment_interactions.py`.
- Streamlit page: `simulator/pages/15_Payment_Interactions.py`.

## Run

```powershell
python scripts/run_payment_interactions.py
```

Generated reports:

- `reports/payment_interactions.md`
- `reports/payment_interactions.json`

## Non-Claims

These are prototype payment-interaction outputs only. They are not UBI policy, welfare advice, eligibility law, Centrelink/DSS/Services Australia modelling, Treasury costing, PBO costing, legal advice, tax advice, or economic validation.

All eligibility, household, targeting, payment, phase-rule, and support-incidence values are illustrative placeholders.

## Future Calibration Needs

Future work requires real welfare, household, population, labour-market, fiscal, legal, privacy, DSS / Services Australia, Treasury, PBO, and economic review before any policy claim or administrative design.
