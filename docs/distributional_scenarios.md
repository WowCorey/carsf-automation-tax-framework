# Synthetic Household Distributional Scenarios

Status: V1.5 prototype synthetic household layer.

## Purpose

This layer previews how different synthetic household archetypes may experience automation-related income shocks, support gaps, payment cliffs, re-employment delays, and regional stress.

It does not use real household data, welfare records, income records, ABS microdata, DSS / Services Australia records, ATO records, HILDA, Census, Treasury, PBO, or household survey data. It does not modify firm-level CARSF liability.

## What Exists

- `model/carsf/households.py` evaluates synthetic household budget stress.
- `model/carsf/reemployment.py` evaluates placeholder re-employment timing.
- `model/carsf/payment_cliffs.py` flags support phase-out or eligibility cliff risk.
- `model/carsf/regional_stress.py` scores placeholder regional stress.
- `model/carsf/distributional_scenarios.py` combines those pieces into household shock bands.
- `model/carsf/distributional_summary.py` summarises synthetic scenario outputs.
- `model/carsf/household_weights.py`, `model/carsf/subgroups.py`, and `model/carsf/weighted_distributional.py` provide a separate synthetic weighting and subgroup aggregation shell.
- Examples live under `examples/distributional_scenarios/`.
- Reports are generated by `python scripts/run_distributional_scenarios.py`.
- Streamlit page: `simulator/pages/16_Distributional_Scenarios.py`.

## Run

```powershell
python scripts/run_distributional_scenarios.py
```

Generated reports:

- `reports/distributional_scenarios.md`
- `reports/distributional_scenarios.json`

Run the separate weighting shell:

```powershell
python scripts/run_household_weighting.py
```

Generated weighting reports:

- `reports/household_weighting.md`
- `reports/household_weighting.json`

## Non-Claims

These are synthetic household distributional scenarios only. They are not real household modelling, welfare advice, eligibility law, DSS/Services Australia modelling, ABS analysis, Treasury modelling, PBO costing, legal advice, tax advice, or economic validation.

All household composition, income, cost, support, regional, re-employment, payment cliff, and shock-band values are illustrative placeholders.

Household weighting outputs are synthetic-only and not representative of any real population.

## Calibration Needs

Future calibration would require ABS, HILDA, Census, DSS / Services Australia, ATO aggregated income/tax, household survey, regional labour-market, retraining access, housing-cost, transport access, Treasury, PBO, legal, privacy, and welfare-administration review.

## Known Risks

- Synthetic scenarios are not population-representative.
- Synthetic weights and subgroup aggregations are not population estimates.
- Household shock bands may overstate precision.
- Payment cliff warnings are prototype labels, not eligibility law.
- Regional stress scores are placeholder-only and not geographic modelling.
- Re-employment timing is not a labour-market forecast.
- Support adequacy cannot be validated without real welfare, household, fiscal, and legal review.
