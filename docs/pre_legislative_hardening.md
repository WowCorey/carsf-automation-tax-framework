# Pre-Build 21 Hardening Pass

This hardening pass prepares the prototype for the later legislative architecture skeleton. It does not add that skeleton, draft operative law, or establish legal sufficiency.

## Scope

- Preserve synthetic subgroup metadata through household weighting, weighted uncertainty, and reviewed-scenario payloads where available.
- Clarify sector stress matrix automation-intensity components by separating digital, physical, decision, and compute metadata signals.
- Tune behavioural response pressure-band spread so synthetic scenarios demonstrate moderate, high, critical, and suppressed pathways without conduct forecasting.
- Add lower-complexity administrative workflow scenarios to show routine and enhanced placeholder routing without real ATO action.

## Non-Claims

- Prototype only.
- Placeholder only.
- Synthetic only where applicable.
- Not calibrated.
- Not legal advice or tax advice.
- Not guidance from the ATO.
- Not Treasury modelling or ABS/ATO/DSS/PBO/HILDA/Census analysis.
- Not real household modelling or population estimates.
- Does not predict conduct or estimate behavioural elasticity.
- Not compliance scoring, enforcement, notices, or penalties.
- Not usable to estimate tax payable in the real world.
- Does not modify firm-level CARSF liability.

## Future Work

After this pass, Build 21 can add a non-operative legislative architecture skeleton showing how CARSF concepts could be organised, while retaining all existing non-claim and suppression boundaries.
