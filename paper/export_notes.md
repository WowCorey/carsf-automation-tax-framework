# Export Notes

The policy paper is maintained in Markdown so it can be versioned, reviewed, and exported later.

## Current Source Files

- `paper/CARSF_V1_4.md` is converted from the supplied PDF.
- `paper/CARSF_V1_5_WORKING.md` is a working copy seeded from V1.4 with V1.5 TODO markers and a release-candidate stack note.
- `release/v1_5_rc/` contains the V1.5 release-candidate pack, including release notes, reviewer briefing, report map, calibration blockers, non-claim boundaries, external-review routing, and a release manifest snapshot.

## Export Requirements

- Preserve status disclaimers.
- Preserve source notes.
- Keep equations readable.
- Do not remove placeholder labels.
- Do not describe V1.5 as final law or government policy.
- Do not describe the release-candidate pack as legal advice, tax advice, ATO guidance, Treasury modelling, economic validation, welfare advice, statistical validation, compliance scoring, enforcement, operational readiness, legal sufficiency, legislative readiness, a readiness score, an official review pathway, or actual-tax-payable analysis.
- Confirm any export does not use real taxpayer, firm-level, industry, ABS, ATO, DSS, Treasury, PBO, HILDA, or Census data and does not modify firm-level CARSF liability.
