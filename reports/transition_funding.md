A national fiscal trajectory can identify displacement and residual gaps, but a human-support layer is needed to preview whether automation revenue could fund displaced-worker supplements, retraining support, UBI-lite, automation-dividend, or hybrid packages. These outputs are not policy recommendations and do not change firm-level liability.

## L. Plain-English Interpretation

### Automation Dividend Placeholder

Automation dividend preview tests broad distribution against stronger placeholder automation capture, without claiming any final dividend design.

- Firm-level CARSF liability is not automatically modified by this transition-funding preview.

### Displaced Worker Supplement Placeholder

Targeted supplement focuses support on net displaced workers and is cheaper than universal designs under placeholder assumptions.

- Firm-level CARSF liability is not automatically modified by this transition-funding preview.

### High Displacement Payment Stress

High displacement paired with low reabsorption generates cliff-risk warnings when payment costs grow faster than placeholder automation revenue coverage.
