# Treasury Methods Review Attacks

This attack document does not mean external review has been completed, does not mean approval has been granted, and does not mean validation has occurred. It is not legal advice, not tax advice, not ATO guidance, not Treasury modelling, not economic validation, not welfare validation, not statistical validation, not compliance scoring, not enforcement, and does not modify firm-level CARSF liability.

## What To Inspect First

- `reports/fiscal_trajectory.md`
- `reports/investment_guardrails.md`
- `reports/transition_funding.md`

## Attack Questions

- Could fiscal outputs look like costing or forecasts?
- Are incidence and investment assumptions clearly placeholders?
- Are pass-through and revenue-capture assumptions exposed?
- Are transition-funding outputs separated from welfare design?
- Are PBO/Treasury-costing overreads blocked?

## Likely Failure Modes

- Fiscal trajectory sounds like a budget estimate.
- Incidence guardrails look empirical.
- Transition funding sounds feasible without review.
- Revenue capture assumptions are hidden.
- Payment and fiscal outputs are combined without caveat.

## Required Evidence / External Review

- Treasury methods review.
- Fiscal methods review.
- Incidence study review.
- Macro assumption review.
- Transition funding policy review.

## What Not To Infer

- Do not infer Treasury modelling, economic validation, forecasts, costing, revenue estimates, approval, validation, or firm-level liability change.

## Locked-Until-Review Items

- Fiscal trajectory assumptions.
- Revenue capture.
- Pass-through.
- Investment deterrence.
- Transition funding feasibility.

## Suggested Reviewer Output Format

Use assumption, report location, fiscal/economic boundary, missing method, and required external review.
