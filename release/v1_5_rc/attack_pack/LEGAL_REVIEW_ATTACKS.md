# Legal Review Attacks

This attack document does not mean external review has been completed, does not mean approval has been granted, and does not mean validation has occurred. It is not legal advice, not tax advice, not ATO guidance, not Treasury modelling, not economic validation, not welfare validation, not statistical validation, not compliance scoring, not enforcement, and does not modify firm-level CARSF liability.

## What To Inspect First

- `reports/legislative_architecture.md`
- `reports/administrative_compliance_workflow.md`
- `reports/mock_evidence_workflow.md`

## Attack Questions

- Could placeholders be read as powers, rights, obligations, penalties, notices, or enforcement?
- Does architecture text drift into operative drafting?
- Are review-right placeholders clearly not real rights?
- Are constitutional and taxing-power questions unresolved?
- Are evidence placeholders kept away from information-gathering powers?

## Likely Failure Modes

- A placeholder looks like a legal test.
- A safeguard looks like a real right.
- A review state looks like legal sufficiency.
- Evidence bundle language sounds like a request under power.
- Regulation placeholders sound available for use.

## Required Evidence / External Review

- Legal review.
- Administrative-law review.
- Constitutional/taxing-power review.
- Privacy/secrecy legal review.
- Parliamentary Counsel review.

## What Not To Infer

- Do not infer legal advice, legal sufficiency, operative law, rights, powers, obligations, notices, enforcement, approval, validation, or firm-level liability change.

## Locked-Until-Review Items

- Evidence powers.
- Review rights.
- Constitutional basis.
- Taxing power.
- Operative drafting.

## Suggested Reviewer Output Format

Use issue, affected text, legal overread risk, required review, and suggested non-operative rewrite.
