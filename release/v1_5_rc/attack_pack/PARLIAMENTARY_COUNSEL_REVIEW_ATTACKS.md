# Parliamentary Counsel Review Attacks

This attack document does not mean external review has been completed, does not mean approval has been granted, and does not mean validation has occurred. It is not legal advice, not tax advice, not ATO guidance, not Treasury modelling, not economic validation, not welfare validation, not statistical validation, not compliance scoring, not enforcement, and does not modify firm-level CARSF liability.

## What To Inspect First

- `reports/legislative_architecture.md`
- `data/legislative_architecture/legislative_architecture_skeleton.yaml`
- `docs/legislative_architecture.md`

## Attack Questions

- Does the Parts and Divisions map stay conceptual?
- Are definition placeholders barred from operative use?
- Do regulation placeholders avoid creating powers?
- Are sector schedules clearly not official schedules?
- Are commencement and transitional placeholders locked?

## Likely Failure Modes

- Architecture text looks enactable.
- Definition placeholder looks usable.
- Schedule placeholder looks official.
- Regulation placeholder looks available.
- Evidence placeholder looks like a statutory power.

## Required Evidence / External Review

- Parliamentary Counsel review.
- Legal drafting review.
- Legislative architecture review.
- Regulation-making review.
- Schedule-authority design review.

## What Not To Infer

- Do not infer drafting sufficiency, legal sufficiency, operative law, rights, powers, obligations, approval, validation, or firm-level liability change.

## Locked-Until-Review Items

- Parts and Divisions.
- Definitions.
- Schedules.
- Regulation placeholders.
- Commencement and transition placeholders.

## Suggested Reviewer Output Format

Use placeholder text, drafting-boundary risk, locked area, and required counsel review.
