This attack document does not mean external review has been completed, does not mean approval has been granted, and does not mean validation has occurred. It is not legal advice, not tax advice, not ATO guidance, not Treasury modelling, not economic validation, not welfare validation, not statistical validation, not compliance scoring, not enforcement, and does not modify firm-level CARSF liability.

## What To Inspect First

- `release/v1_5_rc/NON_CLAIM_BOUNDARIES.md`
- `reports/executive_dashboard.md`

## Attack Questions

- Which sentence could be quoted without its warning?
- Do titles sound more official than contents?
- Is every hard boundary repeated in related reports and docs?
- Does reviewer routing look formal?
- Are stale reports or missing blockers easy to hide?

## Likely Failure Modes

- A sentence sounds like endorsement.
- A heading sounds official.
- A blocker appears in one report but not another.
- A suggested route sounds formal.
- A JSON false flag is absent from Markdown.

## Required Evidence / External Review

- Hostile quote review.
- Forbidden phrase scan.
- Heading review.
- Blocker coverage diff.
- Stale report trace.

## What Not To Infer

- Do not infer approval, validation, legal sufficiency, operational readiness, government endorsement, real policy result, or firm-level liability change.

## Locked-Until-Review Items

- External-review claims.
- Validation language.
- Readiness language.
- Official-status language.
- Firm-liability implications.

## Suggested Reviewer Output Format

Use quote, file, overread risk, violated boundary, suggested rewrite, and required follow-up.
