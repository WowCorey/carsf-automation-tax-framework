# Welfare Policy Review Attacks

This attack document does not mean external review has been completed, does not mean approval has been granted, and does not mean validation has occurred. It is not legal advice, not tax advice, not ATO guidance, not Treasury modelling, not economic validation, not welfare validation, not statistical validation, not compliance scoring, not enforcement, and does not modify firm-level CARSF liability.

## What To Inspect First

- `reports/payment_interactions.md`
- `reports/transition_funding.md`
- `reports/distributional_scenarios.md`

## Attack Questions

- Could payment interactions be mistaken for eligibility law?
- Do household rows look like real hardship evidence?
- Are payment cliffs clearly placeholders?
- Are baseline transfers separated from new support concepts?
- Are DSS/Services Australia boundaries visible?

## Likely Failure Modes

- A payment row looks like an entitlement.
- A household row looks representative.
- Payment cliffs sound calibrated.
- Transition support and baseline support are double counted.
- Welfare administration looks feasible without review.

## Required Evidence / External Review

- Welfare policy review.
- Eligibility-law review.
- DSS/Services Australia methods review.
- Household microsimulation review.
- Payment cliff review.

## What Not To Infer

- Do not infer welfare advice, eligibility law, Services Australia administration, population estimates, welfare validation, approval, validation, or firm-level liability change.

## Locked-Until-Review Items

- Eligibility interactions.
- DSS/Services Australia administration.
- Household hardship validation.
- Payment cliffs.
- Baseline transfer separation.

## Suggested Reviewer Output Format

Use payment or household row, welfare boundary, missing method, external review need, and suggested caveat.
